Understanding Custody Relocation Laws in New York

It's known as parental relocation when a parent seeks to move with a child to another state or significant distance from their current residence. Under New York law, courts evaluate these requests based on the best interests of the child, weighing multiple factors, including:

  • Reasons for Relocation: Is the move driven by economic, employment, educational, or family considerations?
  • Impact on Parent-Child Relationship: How will the move affect the relationship between the child and the non-custodial parent?
  • Quality of Life Improvements: Will the move offer enhanced educational, social, emotional, or financial opportunities?
  • Feasibility of Preserving Visitation: Can practical visitation arrangements still be made for the non-relocating parent?
  • Quality of Life for the Child: Will the relocation significantly improve the child's overall living situation?
