Asset Division & Protecting Your Financial Future

New York follows equitable distribution laws , meaning assets and debts must be divided fairly—but not necessarily equally . Our legal team specializes in:

💰 High-Asset Divorce – Business valuations, real estate, investments, and retirement accounts 💼 Hidden Assets & Fraud Investigations – Ensuring all marital property is properly disclosed 🏡 Marital Home & Property Division – Protecting your fair share in real estate and assets 💳 Debt Allocation – Determining responsibility for joint debts

We aggressively negotiate for fair and favorable asset distribution to help you maintain financial security post-divorce.

Spousal Maintenance & Alimony – Ensuring Fair Support

If there is a financial imbalance between you and your spouse, spousal support (alimony, maintenance) may be necessary. New York courts consider:

💵 Length of the marriage 💵 Income disparity between spouses 💵 Health, education, and employability of each spouse 💵 Contributions to the marriage (including as a homemaker)

We ensure that spousal support awards are fair, whether you are seeking maintenance or contesting an unreasonable demand .
