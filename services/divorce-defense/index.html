Divorce Defense Attorneys in New York

Protecting Your Rights, Reputation, and Assets

Divorce proceedings often begin when one spouse files a case—known as the Plaintiff—leaving the other spouse in the position of Defendant. Though the law treats both parties equally, being the Defendant comes with unique challenges. At Mindin & Mindin, P.C., we specialize in robust divorce defense strategies tailored to your individual circumstances, ensuring your rights and interests are vigorously protected.

What Does It Mean to Defend a Divorce?

When you’re the Defendant in a divorce, you may feel you're on the defensive from the outset. Allegations can be exaggerated, financial claims inflated, and emotions heightened. However, having skilled legal representation means you can effectively counter these issues. A strong defense approach allows you to:

  • Counteract unfair or false accusations
  • Ensure accurate and fair assessments for child and spousal support
  • Safeguard your interests in marital asset distribution

  • Negotiating or litigating fair and realistic support arrangements

  • Aggressively advocate for your rightful share of marital property
