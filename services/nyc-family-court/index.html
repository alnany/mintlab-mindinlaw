Comprehensive Representation for All Family Court Matters

We handle a wide range of Family Court proceedings , including:

📌 Child Custody & Visitation

Custody battles can be stressful, high-stakes, and deeply personal. Whether you are fighting for sole custody, joint custody, or visitation rights, we help parents assert their rights while always prioritizing the best interests of the child.

📌 Child Support Cases

New York law requires both parents to contribute to their child’s financial needs. Whether you are seeking child support or defending against excessive claims, we ensure that child support calculations are fair and accurate, based on New York’s Child Support Standards Act (CSSA).

📌 Orders of Protection

If you are seeking protection from domestic violence or defending against false allegations, our firm aggressively represents clients in order of protection hearings. We understand the impact that a restraining order can have on your custody rights, employment, and future—and we work tirelessly to ensure that your rights are protected.

📌 ACS & Child Neglect Proceedings

If you have been accused of child neglect or abuse in an Administration for Children’s Services (ACS) case, you need an experienced New York Family Court attorney to fight for your parental rights. We defend parents facing investigations, emergency removals, and neglect findings that could impact their custody rights.

📌 Paternity Cases

Paternity cases can affect child support, custody, and visitation rights. Whether you are a father seeking to establish paternity or a mother needing to enforce support, our legal team will guide you through the paternity process in Family Court.

📌 Post-Judgment Modifications & Enforcement

Circumstances change—so do the needs of your family. Whether you need to modify a custody order or child support agreement or enforce an existing court order, we aggressively advocate for fair modifications and compliance with court-ordered obligations.
