While same-sex divorces share many similarities with heterosexual divorces, there are distinct legal complexities that arise—especially in cases involving child custody, parental rights, property division, and spousal maintenance. The legal landscape is still evolving, and ensuring experienced legal representation is crucial to securing a fair and just outcome.

Key Issues in Same-Sex Divorce

Child Custody & Parental Rights

One of the most complex areas in LGBTQ divorce involves child custody and parental rights. While New York law treats same-sex parents equally under custody laws, non-biological or non-adoptive parents may still face challenges in asserting parental rights.

👨‍👨‍👦 Legal Parenthood: If both spouses are legal parents—through adoption or both being listed on the birth certificate—the custody process follows standard New York custody laws.

👩‍👩‍👧 Non-Biological Parents: If one spouse is a biological or adoptive parent and the other is not, custody disputes can become more complicated. Our attorneys fight to protect parental rights and ensure the child’s best interests are upheld.

👩‍❤️‍👩 Third-Party Custody Claims: If one parent has not legally adopted the child, they may face difficulties in custody disputes. We assist in establishing parental rights and advocating for meaningful custody and visitation arrangements.

We are committed to protecting parental rights and ensuring that LGBTQ parents maintain strong, loving relationships with their children after divorce.

Equitable Distribution of Marital Assets

New York follows equitable distribution laws, meaning assets are divided fairly—but not necessarily equally. However, for LGBTQ couples, determining what qualifies as marital property can be more complicated due to the history of legal recognition of same-sex marriages.

💰 Property & Asset Division: If you were together before same-sex marriage was legalized, property accumulated before 2011 may not automatically be considered marital property. This can impact how assets are divided.

📈 Retirement Accounts & Pensions: LGBTQ spouses are entitled to a fair share of 401(k)s, pensions, and retirement savings, but division requires careful legal strategy to avoid tax penalties and ensure fair allocation.

🏡 Real Estate & Home Ownership: Whether you purchased a home together before or after your marriage, our attorneys will ensure a fair and strategic approach to asset division.

Spousal Maintenance (Alimony) in LGBTQ Divorces

Just like in heterosexual divorces, same-sex spouses may be entitled to spousal support (alimony/maintenance) based on factors such as:

✔ Income disparities between spouses ✔ Length of the marriage ✔ Contributions to the household, including as a stay-at-home parent ✔ Health, age, and employability of each spouse

Because same-sex marriage was only legally recognized in 2011, courts may consider long-term relationships that predate marriage differently when determining alimony. We advocate aggressively to ensure fair spousal support awards for our clients.
