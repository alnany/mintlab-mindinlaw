Frequently Asked Questions

How much will my family law or matrimonial case cost? +

Costs vary depending on the complexity of the issues and whether the case is contested; we provide a clear fee structure during your initial consultation based on an hourly rate or a set retainer.

How long does the divorce process typically take in New York? +

An uncontested divorce may be finalized in a few months, while contested matters involving property division or custody disputes can take a year or longer depending on court backlogs.

What documents should I bring to my first meeting with Mindin & Mindin, P.C.? +

You should bring recent tax returns, pay stubs, a list of assets and debts, and any existing court orders or prenuptial agreements relevant to your case.

What are the first legal steps to initiate a case? +

The process typically begins with filing a Summons with Notice or a Verified Complaint in the Supreme Court, or a Petition in Family Court, followed by the formal service of papers to the other party.

Can the firm guarantee a specific outcome regarding custody or asset distribution? +

While no attorney can legally guarantee a specific result, we provide a realistic assessment of your case and use our extensive litigation experience to advocate for your desired goals.

Are there alternatives to going to trial for my family law matter? +

Yes, many of our cases are resolved through negotiated settlements or mediation, which can be a more private, cost-effective, and less adversarial way to reach a final agreement.
